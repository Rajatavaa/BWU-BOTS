# Brightlane Home Services — Services & FAQ (fictional demo company)

## About us
Brightlane Home Services is a small, licensed home-care company. One certified technician
per appointment. All bookings require a customer name, phone, service, date and time.

## Services and prices (visit price, parts/materials billed separately)
- Home Deep Cleaning — 60 min slot — $149
- Standard Home Cleaning — 60 min slot — $89
- Plumbing Visit — 60 min slot — $99
- Electrical Visit — 60 min slot — $99
- HVAC Maintenance — 60 min slot — $129
- Appliance Repair Visit — 60 min slot — $119

## Opening hours and booking rules
- Monday to Saturday, 09:00 to 17:00.
- Start times are on the hour: 09:00, 10:00, 11:00, 12:00, 13:00, 14:00, 15:00, 16:00.
- Closed on Sundays.
- Each appointment occupies one full 60-minute slot with one technician.
- No same-day bookings; appointments must be booked at least one day ahead.

## Service areas and travel fee
- Central district: no travel fee.
- Northide and Riverside zones: $10 travel fee per visit.
- Outside these zones: phone support only.

## Cancellation and reschedule policy
- Free cancellation or reschedule up to 24 hours before the appointment.
- Later than 24 hours: $35 late-change fee.
- No-shows are charged 50% of the visit price.

## Payment
- Payment after the completed service: card or cash.
- Cards on file are optional and stored encrypted.

## Warranty and safety
- 14-day workmanship warranty on all visits.
- Technicians are background-checked, insured and carry ID badges upon arrival.
