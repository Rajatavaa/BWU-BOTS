## Admissions — 9-Step Procedure (admission-procedure.php, faq-admission.php)
1. Select programme (UG/PG/Diploma/PhD)
2. Apply online at /apply-now.php or call Admission 7003162601
3. Get online admission form
4. Fill online admission form (all details except hostel)
5. **Appear & qualify Brainware Entrance Test (BET) — compulsory, 1 hour, 100 marks, MCQ**
6. Send soft-copy documents via mail/WhatsApp to contact person (admission@ / accounts@)
7. Pay via NEFT/IMPS/UPI, send payment details via mail/WhatsApp
8. Complete **Anti-Ragging form** (cell guides)
9. Receive **Admission Letter** on successful completion
- **Booking:** Rs 5,000 provisional seat booking (adjustable against semester fee, non-refundable application fee) = minimum to reserve.
- **Provisional:** Can take admission before Class 12 / graduation results (Aug/Sep), submit docs later, must fulfil eligibility.
- **Correction:** Before admission via form, after admission contact Admission Dept.
- **Documents UG (11 items):** Class 10 admit/marksheet/certificate ×2, Class 12 marksheet ×2, Aadhar ×2, School Leaving ×2, Character recent ×2, Blood group ×1, Caste ×2 if applicable, stamp photo 2, Disability ×2 if applicable.
- **PG (14 items):** Above + Class 12 certificate ×2, Grad marks all years ×2, Grad certificate ×2, Migration original ×1, Blood 2, etc.
- **Diploma (10 items):** Class 10 sets, Aadhar, School Leaving, Character, Blood, Caste, photo, Disability.
- **Foreign (17 items):** Above + Passport notarised ×2, Nationality Certificate NRI/PIO/FN + Guardian for PIO ×2, Physical fitness ×2, plus Equivalence Certificate from UGC via https://equivalence.ugc.ac.in/Home if institution not under MoU, + anti-ragging affidavits (student+parent). Needs Student Visa (5y or course duration, apply at indianvisaonline.gov.in, 6mo passport, photos, admission letter), FRRO registration within 14 days if >180 days stay. Host: 14km from airport. See /foreign-admission.php & Study in India https://studyinindia.gov.in/

## Scholarships (scholarship.php, earlybird-scholarship.php)
